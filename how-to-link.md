# How to link your Discord account with your TowerStats account.

*Published: 2026-02-15*  
*Author: LoveliestJacob*

---

1. In order to link your TowerStats account with your Discord account, you'll first want to login to the website.

!asset login.png

2. After you've logged in, you'll want to open your TowerStats account profile.

!asset open-settings.png

3. Once you've successfully opened your account profile, you will need to enter your Discord username. Then press **save**.

!asset enter-discord.png

**IMPORTANT:** Make sure you enter your Discord **username** and not your display name. You can find your username under your display name on your profile.

!asset discord-username.png

4. Once you have pressed save, head to the [TowerStats Discord server](https://discord.towerstats.com/).

5. After you've joined the Discord server, use the **/connect_discord** command in the **#bot-commands** channel.

!asset connect-discord.png

**IMPORTANT:** Just like your Discord **username**, this field requires your Roblox **username**. **Do not include the @ symbol!**

!asset roblox-username.png

**After that, you are done! You should get a response that says "Connected!".**

!asset success.png

*If you do not get that response, please make sure you followed all the previous steps correctly.*

Can't figure it out? Create a ticket in the Discord server and ask for help! *Note that we cannot complete this process for you.*
